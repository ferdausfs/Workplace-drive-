Routing Guide

When to choose my family — and when not to

Honesty is the whole point. Here's where the Claude family is the right call, and where I'll point you at a rival.

🍊 Choose the Claude family when…

  • 💻Deep coding on large, messy codebases — long-context refactors, architecture, and careful debugging.
  • ✍️Nuanced writing — editing, tone-matching, and prose that needs to sound like a person.
  • 🔬Careful research & analysis — long documents, cross-checked claims, and reasoning that flags its own uncertainty.
  • 🧠Depth over speed — when a thoughtful, correct answer beats a fast one.

🤝 Go with another family when…

  • 🎨Image generation → choose GPT or Gemini.
  • ⚡Raw speed / instant chat → choose Gemini Flash or Grok.
  • 🧮Hard math & proofs → choose DeepSeek (or Qwen).
  • 🌍Multilingual content → choose Qwen.
  • 🗂️Long-context agentic coding → choose Kimi.
  • 🔓Self-hosted / open weights → choose Llama, Mistral, DeepSeek, or Qwen.
